Chocolate Mug Cake

This recipe is perfect for satisfying a sudden sweet craving and cooks in about 90 seconds.

Ingredients

    Dry Ingredients:

  • 3 Tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 3 Tbsp granulated sugar
  • 2 Tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • ¼ tsp baking powder
  • Pinch of salt
  • 3 Tbsp chocolate chips

    • Wet Ingredients:

  • 3 Tbsp milk
  • 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
  • ¼ tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Combine dry ingredients: In a large, microwave-safe mug, thoroughly blend the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt with a fork.
  2. Add wet ingredients: Stir in the milk, oil (or melted butter), and vanilla extract until the batter is smooth, ensuring you scrape the bottom of the mug.
  3. Fold in chocolate chips: Gently mix in the chocolate chips, if using.
  4. Microwave: Cook on high power for approximately 70 to 90 seconds. The cake should look set but still slightly moist on top. Be careful not to overcook it, or it will become dry.
  5. Rest and serve: Allow the mug cake to cool and set for 2-3 minutes before eating. It can be enjoyed straight from the mug or topped with whipped cream or ice cream.
